James Brusey is Professor of Computer Science at the Centre for
Computational Science and Mathematical Methods at Coventry University
and theme lead for Human-centred Reinforcement Learning.
He was
seconded to the Alan Turing Institute as a BridgeAI Independent
Scientific Advisor during 2025. His current research is in machine learning,
reinforcement learning, and applied wireless networked sensing. Recent
projects involve machine learning for heat stress sensing, car cabin
thermal comfort, residential building and thermal comfort, particle
filter algorithms for flow measurement, linear discriminant analysis,
algorithms for network packet reduction, decision support for buildings
monitoring, elderly / infirm falls and near-falls
sensing.

James Brusey received his BASc with distinction and PhD from RMIT
University in 1996 and 2003, respectively. His PhD won the Australian
Computer Science Association award for Best Thesis in 2004. Prior to
joining academia, he had over 15 years experience in the IT industry,
part of which was as an independent
consultant.
